Displacement & Compression Ratio排量与压缩比

Two numbers define an engine's breathing: displacement (how much air it pumps) and compression ratio (how tightly it squeezes it). This tool computes both from the dimensions that matter.两个数字定义了发动机的“呼吸”：排量（泵多少空气）与压缩比（压得多紧）。本工具从关键尺寸算出这两者。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Displacement = (π ÷ 4) × bore² × stroke × cylinders (single-cylinder volume × count)排量 = (π ÷ 4) × 缸径² × 行程 × 缸数（单缸容积 × 缸数）

Clearance volume = displacement ÷ (compression ratio − 1)余隙容积 = 排量 ÷ (压缩比 − 1)

Compression ratio = (displacement + clearance volume) ÷ clearance volume压缩比 = (排量 + 余隙容积) ÷ 余隙容积

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

What is engine displacement?什么是发动机排量？

It is the total swept volume of all cylinders — bore area times stroke times cylinder count. It is the headline 'size' of an engine (e.g. 2.0 L) and the main driver of how much air and fuel it can process.它是所有气缸的总扫掠容积——缸径截面积乘行程再乘缸数。它是发动机的“排量” headline（如 2.0 L），也是决定能处理多少空气与燃油的主要因素。

What is a static compression ratio?什么是静态压缩比？

It is the ratio of the cylinder volume at bottom-dead-centre to the volume at top-dead-centre. It compares the full chamber plus the squeezed clearance volume to the clearance volume alone. Higher generally means more efficiency and power, up to the fuel's knock limit.它是下止点缸内容积与上止点缸内容积之比。它把整个燃烧室加被压缩的余隙容积与余隙容积本身相比。通常越高效率与功率越大，但受燃油抗爆性上限约束。

Why does bore and stroke matter, not just displacement?为什么缸径和行程重要，而不只是排量？

Two engines with the same displacement can feel very different. A long stroke favours low-end torque; a large bore with short stroke favours high-rpm power. The ratio of bore to stroke shapes the engine's character.两台排量相同的发动机手感可能完全不同。长行程偏向低扭；大缸径短行程偏向高转功率。缸径与行程之比塑造了发动机的性格。

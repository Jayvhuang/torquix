Wheel Offset & Backspacing轮毂偏距与内背距

Offset alone is not enough — the same offset on a wider rim sits differently. This tool keeps rim width in the maths so you get the true outer poke and inner clearance, not a guess.光看偏距不够——同样的偏距装到更宽的轮毂上位置就不同了。本工具把轮毂宽度纳入计算，给出真实的向外凸出量和内部间隙，而不是凭感觉估算。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Backspacing = (rim width ÷ 2) + offset (inches)内背距 = (轮毂宽度 ÷ 2) + 偏距（英寸）

Mounting-face position from centreline = offset (positive = outboard, negative = inboard)安装面相对中心线的位置 = 偏距（正 = 偏外，负 = 偏内）

Outer poke = new offset − old offset + (new rim width − old rim width) ÷ 2 (+ spacer on new)向外凸出量 = 新偏距 − 旧偏距 + (新轮毂宽 − 旧轮毂宽) ÷ 2（新轮另加垫片）

Inner clearance = old inner − new inner, where inner = backspacing − rim width内部间隙 = 旧内侧 − 新内侧，其中内侧 = 内背距 − 轮毂宽

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

What is wheel offset?什么是轮毂偏距？

Offset is the distance from the wheel's mounting face to its centreline. Positive offset puts the mounting face toward the outside of the rim (common on FWD cars); negative offset moves it inward (common on trucks and customs).偏距是轮毂安装面到其中心线的距离。正偏距把安装面推向轮毂外侧（前驱车常见）；负偏距把它推向内侧（卡车和改装车常见）。

Does a wider rim change clearance even at the same offset?同样偏距下，更宽的轮毂会改变间隙吗？

Yes. A wider rim at identical offset pushes the outboard lip outward and the inboard lip inward by half the width gain. That is why offset calculators that ignore rim width get it wrong — and why this one requires both.会。同样偏距下，更宽的轮毂会把外侧唇缘向外推、内侧唇缘向内推，各推出宽度增量的一半。这正是为什么忽略轮毂宽度的偏距计算器会算错——本工具因此要求两者都填。

What is backspacing?什么是内背距（backspacing）？

Backspacing is the distance from the mounting face to the back (inboard) lip of the wheel. It is what decides how close the wheel sits to your suspension, brakes and fender liner.内背距是安装面到轮毂后侧（内侧）唇缘的距离。它决定轮毂离你的悬挂、制动和翼子板内衬有多近。

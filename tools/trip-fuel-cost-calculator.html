Trip Fuel Cost行程油费

A road trip's biggest variable cost is fuel. Tell it how far you are going, what the car does on a gallon or litre, and what fuel costs, and it prices the tank stops.自驾游最大的可变成本是油费。告诉它你要走多远、车一加仑或一升能跑多少、油价多少，它就给你算出加油的花费。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Fuel needed = distance ÷ fuel economy所需燃油 = 距离 ÷ 油耗

Cost = fuel needed × price per unit (gallon or litre)费用 = 所需燃油 × 单价（加仑或升）

For mpg input: gallons = miles ÷ mpg; cost = gallons × $/gal若输入 mpg：加仑数 = 英里 ÷ mpg；费用 = 加仑数 × 美元/加仑

Diesel uses the same maths with diesel price柴油用同样的算法，代入柴油价格

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

Does this include tolls and food?这包含过路费和饭钱吗？

No. It prices fuel only — the volume you burn times the price you pay. Tolls, parking and meals are separate and often larger than people expect on a long trip.不包含。它只算油费——你烧掉的油量乘以单价。过路费、停车和吃饭是另算的，而且在长途里往往比人们预期的多。

Should I use real-world or sticker mpg?该用实际油耗还是标称油耗？

Use a real-world figure if you have one. Highway trips often beat the combined sticker number, while loaded cars, roof boxes and mountains can fall well short of it.如果有实际数据就用实际的。高速行程常常优于综合标称值，而满载、车顶箱和山路则可能远不及标称。

How do I handle diesel?柴油怎么处理？

Diesel is the same maths — volume times price — just use the diesel pump price, which is often higher per litre than petrol even when a diesel goes farther per litre.柴油算法一样——体积乘价格——只是代入柴油泵价。即便柴油每升跑得更远，泵价每升往往也比汽油高。

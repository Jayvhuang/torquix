Tire Size Comparison轮胎尺寸对比

Putting a different size on the car changes far more than the look — it moves your speedometer, your gearing and your fender clearance. This tool draws both sizes to scale and gives you the numbers that actually change.给车换一个不同尺寸的轮胎，改变的不只是外观——它还会移动你的码表、齿比和翼子板间隙。本工具按比例画出两种尺寸，并给出真正会变化的数字。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Overall diameter = section width × (aspect ÷ 100) × 2 + rim diameter × 25.4 (mm)外径 = 断面宽 × (扁平比 ÷ 100) × 2 + 轮毂直径 × 25.4（毫米）

Sidewall height = section width × (aspect ÷ 100) (mm)胎壁高度 = 断面宽 × (扁平比 ÷ 100)（毫米）

Rolling circumference = π × overall diameter; revs per mile = 63,360 ÷ circumference (in)滚动周长 = π × 外径；每英里转数 = 63,360 ÷ 周长（英寸）

Diameter change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100%直径变化 = (新 − 旧) ÷ 旧 × 100%

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

How much bigger can a tire be before it hurts?一套轮胎可以比原厂大多少才不会出问题？

Most shops and manufacturers treat a 3% change in overall diameter as the safe limit. Within ±3% your speedometer stays close, your gearing does not drift badly, and the tire is unlikely to rub. Above that, expect a noticeable speedo error and possible clearance problems.大多数门店和厂家把外径变化 ±3% 视为安全上限。在 ±3% 以内，码表偏差很小、齿比不会明显漂移，轮胎也不太会蹭到。超过这个范围，码表误差会变得明显，并可能出现间隙问题。

Why does my speedometer read wrong after a tire change?为什么换了轮胎后码表读不准？

The speed sensor counts wheel revolutions and multiplies by the tire's circumference the car was built with. A larger tire covers more ground per revolution, so the indicated speed reads lower than your true speed; a smaller tire reads high.速度传感器统计车轮转数，再乘以车辆出厂时设定的轮胎周长。更大的轮胎每转走得更远，所以显示速度比真实速度低；更小的轮胎则显示偏高。

What does revs per mile mean?“每英里转数”是什么意思？

It is how many times a wheel turns to cover one mile. It decides engine RPM at a given speed, odometer pace and how a change in tire size affects both. Lower revs per mile usually means lower cruising RPM.就是车轮走完一英里需要转多少圈。它决定某一车速下的发动机转速、里程表节奏，以及换胎尺寸会如何影响这两者。每英里转数越低，通常巡航转速也越低。

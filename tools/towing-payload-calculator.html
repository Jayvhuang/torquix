Towing & Payload Check拖挂与载重核算

Towing numbers are easy to exceed and hard to recover from. This tool checks your payload, tongue weight and combined limit against the door-jamb ratings so you know you are legal and safe.拖挂数值很容易被超限，而且很难补救。本工具把你的载重、舌重和总上限与门框铭牌对比，让你确认既合法又安全。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Payload capacity = GVWR − curb weight (including passengers and cargo)载重能力 = GVWR − 整备质量（含乘客与货物）

Tongue weight ≈ 10–15% of trailer weight (trailer GVWR)舌重 ≈ 拖车重量（拖车 GVWR）的 10–15%

Loaded vehicle weight = curb + passengers + cargo + tongue weight装载后车重 = 整备 + 乘客 + 货物 + 舌重

Remaining towing capacity = GCWR − loaded vehicle weight剩余拖拽能力 = GCWR − 装载后车重

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

What is the difference between GVWR and GCWR?GVWR 和 GCWR 有什么区别？

GVWR is the maximum your vehicle may weigh on its own axle (curb + people + cargo + tongue). GCWR is the maximum of vehicle plus trailer together. Both are printed on the door jamb and both must be respected.GVWR 是车辆自身轴系允许的最大重量（整备 + 人 + 货物 + 舌重）。GCWR 是车辆加拖车的总和上限。两者都印在门框上，且都必须遵守。

How much tongue weight should a trailer have?拖车应该有多少舌重？

Typically 10–15% of the loaded trailer weight for a conventional bumper-pull. Too little and the trailer can sway; too much and you eat into the vehicle's payload and overload the rear axle.对普通拖杠式拖车，通常为满载拖车重的 10–15%。太少会摆尾，太多则吃掉车辆载重并压坏后轴。

Does the trailer's weight count against my payload?拖车的重量会占用我的载重吗？

Yes — through the tongue. The tongue weight sits on the vehicle and must fit inside GVWR along with passengers and cargo, which is the limit most people forget.会——通过舌重。舌重压在车辆上，必须和乘客、货物一起纳入 GVWR，而这正是大多数人会忘记的限制。

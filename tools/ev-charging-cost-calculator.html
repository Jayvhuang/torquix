EV Charging Cost & Time电动车充电成本与时间

Charging an EV is just buying kilowatt-hours, but the time depends on the charger. This tool prices the session and tells you how long the plug stays in.给电动车充电其实就是买千瓦时，但时间取决于充电桩。本工具给这次充电定价，并告诉你插枪要插多久。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Energy needed = battery capacity × (target% − start%) ÷ 100 (kWh)所需电量 = 电池容量 × (目标% − 起始%) ÷ 100（kWh）

Cost = energy needed × price per kWh费用 = 所需电量 × 每 kWh 价格

Time = energy needed ÷ charger power (allow ~90% efficiency)时间 = 所需电量 ÷ 充电桩功率（预留约 90% 效率）

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

Why is charging slower near full?为什么快充满时充电变慢？

Batteries accept current fastest in the middle of their range and the charger tapers the rate as the pack fills, to protect cells and balance temperature. The last 10–20% can take as long as the first 60–70%.电池在电量中段接受电流最快，而充电桩会在电池充满过程中逐步降低速率，以保护电芯、平衡温度。最后 10–20% 可能和前 60–70% 花一样久。

Does the price per kWh include everything?每 kWh 价格包含一切吗？

It prices the electricity at the rate you enter. Public DC fast chargers often bill a session or per-minute fee on top, and some add idle fees, so a public stop can cost more than the energy alone.它按你输入的电价给电能定价。公共直流快充常在电费之外再收会话费或按时费，有些还有占位费，所以公共充电可能比单纯电能更贵。

What is a good home rate to use?家用该用哪个电价？

Use your domestic electricity tariff per kWh. Many EVs charge at home overnight on off-peak rates, which is typically far cheaper per mile than petrol — but rates vary a lot by region.用你家每 kWh 的电价。很多电动车夜里在家用谷电充电，每英里通常比汽油便宜得多——但各地电价差异很大。

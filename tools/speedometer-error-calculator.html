Speedometer & Odometer Error码表与里程表误差

Your gauge cluster does not know you changed tires. Enter the before and after diameters and this tool shows what your speedometer and odometer are really telling you.你的仪表盘并不知道你换了轮胎。输入换胎前后的外径，本工具就会告诉你码表和里程表到底显示了什么。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Ratio = new overall diameter ÷ old overall diameter比值 = 新外径 ÷ 旧外径

True speed = indicated speed × ratio真实速度 = 显示速度 × 比值

Speed error = (ratio − 1) × 100%速度误差 = (比值 − 1) × 100%

Odometer error follows the same ratio (recorded miles = true miles ÷ ratio)里程表误差遵循同一比值（记录里程 = 真实里程 ÷ 比值）

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

Does a bigger tire make the speedometer read high or low?更大的轮胎会让码表读高还是读低？

Low. A taller tire travels farther per revolution, so the cluster — still counting the old circumference — under-reports your true speed. You are actually going faster than it shows.读低。更高的轮胎每转走得更远，而仪表盘仍按旧周长计数，于是低估了你的真实速度。你实际开得比它显示的更快。

How do I read my true speed from the dash?怎么从仪表盘读出真实速度？

Multiply the displayed speed by the diameter ratio (new ÷ old). At a 5% larger tire and a 60 mph reading, your true speed is about 63 mph.把显示速度乘以直径比值（新 ÷ 旧）。若轮胎大了 5%、表显 60 mph，真实速度约为 63 mph。

Does the odometer drift the same way?里程表也会以同样方式漂移吗？

Yes, by the same ratio. Over many miles a larger tire under-counts distance, so your recorded mileage will read lower than the ground you actually covered.会，按同一比值。长途下来，更大的轮胎会少计里程，所以记录里程会比你实际走过的路更短。

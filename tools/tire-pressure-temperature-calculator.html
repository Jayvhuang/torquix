Tire Pressure & Temperature胎压与温度换算

A cold-morning low-pressure light is often just physics. This tool applies the gas law so you can predict what your tires will read after a temperature swing, without guessing.冷天早晨亮起的低压灯往往只是物理现象。本工具套用气体定律，让你无需猜测就能预测温度变化后胎压会读多少。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

P₂ = P₁ × (T₂_abs ÷ T₁_abs) (Gay-Lussac's law, pressures absolute)P₂ = P₁ × (T₂绝对 ÷ T₁绝对)（盖-吕萨克定律，压力取绝对压力）

Absolute temp = Celsius + 273.15 = (Fahrenheit + 459.67) ÷ 1.8绝对温度 = 摄氏 + 273.15 = (华氏 + 459.67) ÷ 1.8

Gauge pressure = absolute pressure − atmospheric (≈14.7 psi)表压 = 绝对压力 − 大气压（约 14.7 psi）

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

Why does my TPMS light come on when it gets cold?为什么天冷时 TPMS 灯会亮？

Air contracts as it cools, so pressure drops with temperature — about 1 psi per 10°F (roughly 1.8 kPa per 5°C). A tire set at 35 psi warm can read low on a cold morning and trigger the light.空气遇冷收缩，所以压力随温度下降——约每 10°F 降 1 psi（约每 5°C 降 1.8 kPa）。35 psi 的热胎在冷早晨可能读偏低，从而触发警示灯。

Should I add air when the light is on cold?冷车时灯亮了要补气吗？

If the tire is actually below its placard pressure when cold, yes — top it to the placard and recheck. But do not chase the warm reading; the placard pressure is a cold specification.如果冷车时轮胎确实低于铭牌压力，要——补到铭牌值再复查。但不要去追热车读数；铭牌压力是冷车标准。

Is the gas law exact for tires?气体定律对轮胎完全精确吗？

It is a good approximation for the air's pressure–temperature relation at fixed volume. Real tires flex and heat as you drive, so on-road pressure rises above the cold calculation — which is why you set pressure cold.在定容下，它是空气压力-温度关系的一个很好的近似。真实轮胎行驶时会形变生热，所以路上压力会高于冷车计算值——这正是为什么要在冷车时设定压力。

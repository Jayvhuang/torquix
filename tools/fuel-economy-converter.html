Fuel Economy Converter油耗单位换算

The world measures fuel economy at least four different ways. This tool flips between them so a US mpg figure and a European L/100 km figure finally mean the same thing.这个世界至少用四种方式衡量油耗。本工具在它们之间切换，让美制 mpg 和欧洲 L/100 km 终于表示同一件事。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

L/100 km = 235.215 ÷ mpg(US)L/100 km = 235.215 ÷ mpg(美制)

mpg(US) = 235.215 ÷ L/100 kmmpg(美制) = 235.215 ÷ L/100 km

mpg(UK) = mpg(US) × 1.20095 (UK gallon = 4.546 L vs US 3.785 L)mpg(英制) = mpg(美制) × 1.20095（英制加仑 4.546 L，美制 3.785 L）

km/L = 100 ÷ L/100 kmkm/L = 100 ÷ L/100 km

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

Why are US and UK mpg different?为什么美制与英制 mpg 不一样？

They use different gallons. A US gallon is 3.785 L; a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 L. So the same real efficiency shows a higher number in UK mpg — about 20% higher — which is a constant source of confusion.它们用的是不同的加仑。美制加仑 3.785 升，英制（帝国）加仑 4.546 升。所以同样的真实效率在英制 mpg 下数字更高——约高 20%——这经常让人混淆。

Is a lower L/100 km better?L/100 km 越低越好吗？

Yes. Litres per 100 km is a direct consumption figure: less fuel per distance is better. It is the opposite of mpg, where a higher number is better.是的。升/100 公里是直接的消耗量：每距离消耗的燃油越少越好。这与 mpg 相反，mpg 是越高越好。

What is km/L used for?km/L 用来做什么？

It is common in parts of Asia and shows how many kilometres one litre takes you. It sits between mpg and L/100 km in feel: higher is better, like mpg.在亚洲部分地区常见，表示一升油能跑多少公里。它的“手感”介于 mpg 与 L/100 km 之间：和 mpg 一样越高越好。

Gear Ratio & Cruising RPM齿比与巡航转速

Engine RPM at highway speed is set by three things: your gearing, your tire diameter and your speed. Change any one and the RPM moves. This tool shows where yours lands.高速巡航时的发动机转速由三件事决定：齿比、轮胎直径和车速。改变其中任何一项，转速都会变。本工具告诉你落在哪个转速。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

RPM = (speed in mph × 336 × axle ratio × gear ratio) ÷ tire diameter (inches)转速 = (车速 mph × 336 × 主减速比 × 挡位齿比) ÷ 轮胎直径（英寸）

For km/h: RPM = (speed × 209.2 × axle × gear) ÷ diameter(in)公里/小时：转速 = (车速 × 209.2 × 主减速比 × 齿比) ÷ 直径（英寸）

Taller tire → lower RPM at the same speed; shorter tire → higher RPM轮胎越高 → 同速下转速越低；轮胎越矮 → 转速越高

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

What is the 336 constant?336 这个常数是什么？

It folds together the inches in a mile (63,360) and the circumference factor so you can solve RPM from mph, gear ratios and tire diameter in inches in one step. The km/h version uses 209.2.它把一英里的英寸数（63,360）和周长系数合并在一起，让你一步从 mph、各齿比和轮胎英寸直径解出转速。公里/小时版本用 209.2。

Will bigger tires lower my RPM?更大的轮胎会降低我的转速吗？

Yes. A taller tire covers more ground per engine revolution, so at the same road speed the engine turns slower. That can improve fuel use and reduce noise, but can also blunt acceleration.会。更高的轮胎每转发动机多走一段路，所以同速下发动机转得更慢。这能改善油耗、降低噪音，但也可能削弱加速。

What RPM should I cruise at?巡航转速应该多少？

It depends on the engine, but many street engines are happiest cruising between roughly 1,800 and 2,800 rpm on the highway. Very low RPM can lug the engine; very high RPM wastes fuel and makes noise.取决于发动机，但很多道路发动机在高速巡航时最喜欢约 1,800 到 2,800 rpm 之间。转速过低会拖挡，过高则费油又吵。

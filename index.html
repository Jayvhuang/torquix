How Torquix works

Every tool is the same three moves: turn a spec code into real geometry, apply the physics, then translate it back into something you can act on.

1. Decode the spec

225/45R17 is not a size, it is three numbers in three different units. Torquix converts sidewall percentages, rim inches and section millimetres into one honest diameter before anything else happens.

2. Apply the physics

Rolling circumference drives speedometer error and gearing. Offset and rim width place the wheel relative to the hub face. Gay-Lussac's law moves tire pressure with temperature. Same equations a workshop uses.

3. Make it a decision

A number is useless until it is a yes or a no. Torquix flags the ±3% diameter rule, tongue-weight ranges, TPMS trigger thresholds and cost-per-mile so you leave with an answer, not homework.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I change tire diameter safely?轮胎外径最多能改多少？

The widely used industry guideline is to stay within 3% of the original overall diameter. Inside that band the speedometer stays close enough, ABS and stability-control wheel-speed comparisons still behave, and an all-wheel-drive centre differential is not fighting a permanent mismatch. Torquix flags the moment you cross 3% and again at 5%, where the effects stop being subtle.行业通行的建议是把外径变化控制在原车的 3% 以内。在这个范围内，码表误差可以接受，ABS 和车身稳定系统的轮速比对仍然正常，四驱车的中央差速器也不会长期处于持续打滑状态。Torquix 会在超过 3% 时给出提示，超过 5% 时再次警告——那时候各种影响就不再是「轻微」了。

Why do most wheel offset calculators give the wrong answer?为什么多数偏距计算器算出来是错的？

Because they compare offsets without asking for rim width. Offset is measured from the wheel's centreline, so a 9-inch wheel at ET35 sits half an inch further out than an 8-inch wheel at the same ET. Torquix asks for both widths, both offsets, spacer thickness and both tire section widths, then reports outer poke and inner clearance separately — because those two numbers hit different parts of the car.因为它们只比偏距，却不问轮辋宽度。偏距是从轮毂中心线量起的，所以同样 ET35，9 英寸轮毂会比 8 英寸轮毂多外抛约半英寸。Torquix 会同时要求两个宽度、两个偏距、垫片厚度和两条胎宽，并把「外抛量」和「内侧间隙」分开报告——因为这两个数字影响的是车上完全不同的部位。

Is my speedometer wrong even with stock tires?原厂胎的码表也会不准吗？

Almost certainly, and deliberately. UNECE Regulation 39 and EU Directive 2000/7/EC permit a speedometer to read up to 10% plus 4 km/h above true speed, but never below it. That is why a GPS check on a completely stock car usually shows you travelling a little slower than the dial claims — and it is also why fitting slightly taller tires can, by coincidence, make the reading more accurate rather than less.几乎一定不准，而且是故意的。联合国 UNECE R39 和欧盟 2000/7/EC 允许码表读数比真实车速高出 10% + 4 km/h，但绝不允许偏低。所以哪怕是完全原厂的车，用 GPS 一测通常也会比码表慢一点——这也解释了为什么换上略大一点的轮胎，有时反而会让码表读数更接近真实值。

Why does the tire pressure light come on when it gets cold?为什么一降温胎压灯就亮？

Gauge pressure follows absolute temperature, not the number on the gauge. Cooling a tire from 21°C to 4°C drops it by roughly 1.5 to 2 psi, so a car set slightly low in autumn can cross the warning threshold on the first frosty morning. Under FMVSS 138 that light fires at 25% below the placard pressure — Torquix works out exactly where that point is for your car.表压跟随的是绝对温度，而不是表盘上的数字。轮胎从 21°C 降到 4°C，胎压大约下降 1.5–2 psi，所以秋天本来就打得偏低的车，第一个霜冻早晨就可能跨过报警线。按 FMVSS 138，报警点是铭牌胎压的 75%——Torquix 会替你算出这个具体数值。

Are these numbers exact for my specific vehicle?这些数字对我的车精确吗？

The geometry and physics are exact for what you type, but a real car adds variables Torquix cannot see. A loaded tire has a smaller rolling radius than its theoretical diameter, brake calipers and fender liners decide real clearance, and two brands of the same size code can differ by several millimetres. Treat the output as a well-founded prediction, then verify on the car before you cut, buy or tow.对你输入的数值而言，几何和物理是精确的；但真实车辆还有 Torquix 看不到的变量。受载轮胎的滚动半径小于理论外径，实际间隙由卡钳和翼子板内衬决定，同一规格不同品牌之间也可能差好几毫米。请把结果当作有依据的预测，在开工、下单或挂车之前，仍要到车上实测。

Does anything I type get uploaded?我输入的内容会被上传吗？

No. Every calculation runs in JavaScript inside your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored anywhere. Only your language, theme and unit-system preferences are saved in local storage on your own device, and you can clear those at any time.不会。所有计算都在你浏览器里用 JavaScript 完成，输入内容不会发送到任何服务器，也不会被保存。只有语言、主题和单位制偏好会存在你自己设备的本地存储里，随时可以清除。

Can I use Torquix in metric and in Chinese?支持公制和中文吗？

Yes. One switch converts every input and result between US units and metric, and it converts the values you have already typed instead of wiping them. The entire interface — result labels, formula notes, warning flags — switches between English and 简体中文 independently of the unit system, so you can read Chinese while working in inches, or the reverse.支持。一个开关就能把所有输入和结果在英制与公制之间切换，而且是换算你已经填好的数值，而不是清空重填。整个界面——结果标签、公式说明、警示提示——都能在英文和简体中文之间切换，且与单位制互相独立：你完全可以用中文界面配英寸单位，反之亦然。

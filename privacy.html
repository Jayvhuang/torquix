Privacy Policy隐私政策

Last updated: 2026. Torquix is a set of calculators that run entirely in your web browser. This page explains what data leaves your device, what stays on it, and what third parties may collect when you use the site.最后更新：2026 年。Torquix 是一组完全在你的浏览器中运行的计算器。本页说明哪些数据会离开你的设备、哪些留在本地，以及使用本站点时第三方可能收集哪些信息。

1. What we do NOT collect1. 我们不收集什么

Every calculator on Torquix runs locally in JavaScript. The numbers you type — tire sizes, offset, fuel prices, weights, pressures — are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers. We do not operate user accounts, and we do not ask you to sign up. We have no database of your inputs, and we cannot see what you calculate.Torquix 上的每个计算器都在本地 JavaScript 中运行。你输入的数字——轮胎尺寸、偏距、油价、重量、胎压——都在你自己的设备上处理，绝不会上传到我们的服务器。我们不提供用户账号，也不要求你注册。我们没有你输入的数据库，也无法看到你计算了什么。

2. Third-party cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)2. 第三方 Cookie 与广告（Google AdSense）

Torquix may serve advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use third-party cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. These cookies are set by Google, not by Torquix.Torquix 可能通过 Google AdSense 投放广告。Google 及其合作伙伴可能使用第三方 Cookie（包括 DoubleClick Cookie），根据你对本站或其他网站的先前访问来投放广告。这些 Cookie 由 Google 设置，而非由 Torquix 设置。

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or by visiting aboutads.info. Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by its advertising partners policy.你可以通过访问 Google 广告设置 或 aboutads.info 选择退出个性化广告。Google 对广告 Cookie 的使用受其广告合作伙伴政策的约束。

Google's advertising partners policy, including how Google and its partners use data, is described here: https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/Google 的广告合作伙伴政策（包括 Google 及其合作伙伴如何使用数据）在此说明：https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/

3. Web beacons and similar technologies3. 网络信标与类似技术

Advertising and analytics partners may use web beacons (also called pixel tags or clear GIFs) — small invisible images embedded in pages or ads — to measure impressions, count ad views, and attribute conversions. A web beacon can record that your browser loaded a particular page and may read a cookie previously set by the same partner. Torquix itself does not embed tracking beacons for its own analytics.广告和分析合作伙伴可能使用网络信标（也称像素标签或透明 GIF）——嵌入页面或广告中的小型不可见图像——来衡量曝光量、统计广告浏览次数并归因转化。网络信标可以记录你的浏览器加载了某个页面，并可能读取同一合作伙伴先前设置的 Cookie。Torquix 自身不会为了自己的分析而嵌入跟踪信标。

4. IP address and server logs4. IP 地址与服务器日志

When you request any page, our hosting provider's web server records your IP address, the requested URL, browser type, referring page and a timestamp in its access logs. This is standard web-server behaviour used for security, abuse prevention and aggregate traffic reporting. We do not use these logs to reconstruct your individual calculations.当你请求任何页面时，我们托管服务商的 Web 服务器会在访问日志中记录你的IP 地址、请求的 URL、浏览器类型、来源页面和时间戳。这是标准的 Web 服务器行为，用于安全、防滥用和整体流量统计。我们不会用这些日志去还原你的个人计算内容。

5. Affiliate links (Amazon Associates)5. 联盟链接（Amazon Associates）

Torquix is a participant in the Amazon Associates programme. Some links to products (tyre gauges, torque wrenches, air compressors, etc.) are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, Amazon may set its own cookies and may associate the purchase with our associate ID. This does not change the price you pay. Amazon's privacy practices are governed by its own policy: amazon.com/privacy.Torquix 是 Amazon Associates 联盟计划的参与者。部分指向商品的链接（胎压计、扭力扳手、充气泵等）是联盟链接。如果你点击并购买，Amazon 可能会设置自己的 Cookie，并将购买关联到我们的联盟 ID。这不会改变你支付的价格。Amazon 的隐私实践受其自身政策约束：amazon.com/privacy。

6. Your choices6. 你的选择

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking third-party cookies may disable personalised ads but will not affect the calculators — all of them work without any cookies. To control Google's use of your data for advertising, use the links in section 2.你可以随时在浏览器设置中阻止或删除 Cookie。阻止第三方 Cookie 可能会关闭个性化广告，但不会影响计算器——所有计算器都无需任何 Cookie 即可运行。要控制 Google 将你的数据用于广告，请使用第 2 节中的链接。

7. Children7. 儿童

Torquix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.Torquix 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

8. Contact8. 联系方式

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@torquix.com.有关本政策的疑问可发送至 privacy@torquix.com。
